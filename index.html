About the Founder

M. Shajitha Rahima

Founder — Reboot Mental Health Center · Psychologist · Counselling & Psychotherapy Professional

M. Shajitha Rahima is a psychology professional, mental-health practitioner, educator, and founder with more than 10 years of experience in counselling, psychotherapy, student wellbeing, life-skills education, training, and psychological interventions.

Her professional journey spans schools, colleges, and community settings, where she has worked with children, adolescents, educators, and families — conducting 100+ workshops and delivering counselling and psychological interventions across CBT, REBT, Behaviour Modification, Expressive Art Therapy, NLP, Group Counselling, and Life Skills.

Driven by the belief that psychology should extend beyond the counselling room, she founded Reboot Mental Health Center — a platform focused on making mental-health support, psychological education, life skills, and wellbeing more accessible, practical, and meaningful for everyday life.

Mental Health Camp Motto

Mental Health Is Not a Luxury. It Is a Right.

Mental health is not a privilege reserved for those who can afford it. It is a fundamental part of being human — just as essential as physical health. Yet, stigma, misconceptions, and the belief that mental health care is "too expensive" continue to keep people from seeking the support they deserve.

Through this Mental Health Camp, Reboot Mental Health Center takes a step towards changing that narrative.

We believe that seeking help is not a sign of weakness. It is an act of courage, awareness, and self-care. Every person deserves access to professional, compassionate, and quality mental health care, regardless of their background or financial status.

This camp is more than an initiative — it is a statement of what mental health care in Tiruppur can and should be.

Reboot Mental Health Center is committed to setting a new standard for accessible mental health care in Tiruppur — where seeking help is normal, care is affordable, and no one is left behind.

Because mental health is not a luxury. It is the first step towards a healthier, stronger, and more meaningful life.
