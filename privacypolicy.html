Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

Reboot Mental Health Care respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information provided by participants during registration for our mental health camp.

1. Information We Collect

During registration, we may collect information required to process your camp registration and communicate with you.

This may include:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Registration details
  • Payment and transaction-related information
  • Other information voluntarily provided during registration

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected may be used for purposes directly related to the camp, including:

  • Processing your camp registration
  • Confirming your registration and payment
  • Communicating important camp information
  • Providing updates regarding the camp schedule or arrangements
  • Responding to participant questions and requests
  • Maintaining registration and administrative records

3. No Marketing Data Sharing

Your privacy is important to us.

Reboot Mental Health Care does not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Your registration information will not be provided to third parties for their own promotional, advertising or marketing activities.

4. Payment Information

Payments are processed through the payment gateway used on the registration website.

Reboot Mental Health Care does not intentionally collect or store sensitive payment credentials such as card PINs, CVVs or banking passwords.

Payment processing may be subject to the privacy policy and terms of the payment service provider.

5. Website Management

The registration website is technically handled by Palette Production on behalf of Reboot Mental Health Care.

Palette Production may process information only to the extent necessary to operate, maintain and support the registration website and related technical services.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information submitted through the registration website against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Communication

We may use the contact information provided during registration to send important communications related to your camp registration, payment, schedule, venue, timings or other necessary camp updates.

These communications are considered service-related communications and are not intended to be third-party marketing communications.

8. Data Retention

Registration information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for registration management, administrative records, payment reconciliation, legal requirements and other legitimate operational purposes.

9. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services such as payment gateways, hosting providers or technical infrastructure providers to operate the registration system.

Such services may process information as necessary to provide their respective services and may have their own terms and privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Registration should be completed by an individual who is legally permitted to provide the information. Where registration involves a minor, the registration should be completed with the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian where required.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Reboot Mental Health Care may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any updated version may be published on this website with a revised "Last Updated" date.

12. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your registration information is handled, you may contact Reboot Mental Health Care through the contact details provided on the official registration website, or call us directly at +91 99948 71126.
